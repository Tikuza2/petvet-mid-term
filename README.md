# PetVet (Mid-Term)

Create a modern, responsive web application for "PetVet Georgia" – a digital veterinary clinic and pet health platform.

Design System:

- Palette: Eco Green (#2ECC71), Soft Sage background (#F4F7F6), Dark Slate text (#1E293B), Accent Orange (#FF7675) for CTAs.

- Clean, rounded corners (rounded-xl), spacious layout, intuitive typography.

Structure & Sections:

1. Header / Navbar:

   - Logo: "PetVet Georgia" with a small paw icon.

   - Nav Links: Home, Find Vets, Services, Pet Passport, About Us.

   - CTA Button: "Book Appointment".

2. Hero Section:

   - Headline: "Healthcare Your Pet Deserves, Made Effortless."

   - Subheadline: "Book top-rated veterinarians in Georgia, manage digital medical passports, and get smart reminders 24/7."

   - Search bar with filters: Location (Tbilisi, Batumi), Service (General Checkup, Vaccination, Dentistry), and Date.

   - Quick statistics badge: "5,000+ Happy Pets | 120+ Verified Vets | 4.9 ★ Rating".

3. Services Grid:

   - Cards with hover effects for: General Consultation, Emergency Care, Vaccination & Microchipping, Pet Grooming.

4. Featured Vets / Doctor Catalog:

   - Interactive doctor cards showing photo, name, specialization, experience years, rating, and an inline "Book Time Slot" calendar.

5. Digital Pet Passport Preview (Interactive Component):

   - A visual card showing a pet profile (e.g., "Rocky - French Bulldog, 2 yrs"), upcoming vaccination date, medical history log, and downloadable PDF report button.

6. Booking Modal / Form:

   - Step-by-step interactive booking form: Choose Pet -> Choose Service -> Select Date & Time -> Contact Info -> Confirmation Toast.

7. Testimonials & Footer:

   - User review cards and a full dark footer with quick links, contacts, emergency hotline, and social icons.
